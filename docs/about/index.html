About

Impact, across domains

Paul Raff — who goes by Raff — has over 15 years of experience in data science and applied research on top of his PhD education. Here's a summary of the impact he has delivered across multiple domains.

Reddit

Principal Data Scientist

Raff joined Reddit in 2021 and had immediate impact with both the Reddit Search product team and Reddit's Experimentation Platform, DDG. He created the foundation for Search's data strategy, enabling the Search engineering team and the rest of Reddit to effectively utilize its data for maximum insight and business value. In parallel, he architected the modernization of DDG's analysis platform, growing Reddit's experimentation throughput from tens to hundreds of simultaneous experiments. Raff is one of only two Principal Data Scientists at Reddit — the highest individual-contributor role — and part of Reddit's technical leadership.

Default Open ↗

Aetna, a CVS Health Company

Principal Data Scientist — Clinical Analytics

Raff came in at a critical time in the development of Next-Generation Transform Diabetes Care (ngTDC) and took ownership of the technical aspects of the core machine learning models underlying the program. Working alongside Product, Engineering, Sales, and IT, he led a team of 5 Data Scientists to complete the on-time launch of the pilot and the full commercial launch — contributing hundreds of millions of dollars of EBIT for CVS Health and providing a solid foundation for how CVS Health and Aetna use data to manage chronic diseases.

Next-Generation Transform Diabetes Care ↗

Microsoft

Principal Data Scientist Lead — ExP

Principal Program Manager Lead — COSINE Data

Raff cut his teeth in A/B experimentation in Microsoft's ExP org, helping it grow from an organization of 10 to 150 — starting as an individual contributor and ending as the leader of a team of 10. He helped Bing, Windows, Office, Xbox, and other organizations run tens of thousands of A/B experiments with simple, effective, and trustworthy analysis. Raff played instrumental roles in ensuring experimentation was built into the core of Windows 10, and built tools to help experimenters understand results and iterate quickly.

ExP ↗

Amazon

Applied Researcher — Supply Chain Optimization

Raff's contribution to supply chain optimization at Amazon resulted in $60 million of fulfillment savings for Amazon's network in 2012 — think about what that means for Amazon today. He did it through a simple and effective technique coined Global Fulfillment Optimization. Building on patented ideas on fulfillment tie-breaking, he worked with other scientists and engineers to reshape and advance Amazon's fulfillment strategies to massive effect.
